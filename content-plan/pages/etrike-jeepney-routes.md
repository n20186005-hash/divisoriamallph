## 3. Strategy
For short hops around Divisoria, **jeepneys and e-trikes** fill the gaps trains don't. But for distances under ~500 m, **walking beats bargaining** with a trike. Use rides mainly to cross between hubs (mall ↔ 999/168 ↔ Binondo) with heavy bags.

## 4. Options (examples)
1. **Jeepney along Tabora/Recto** — to Quiapo, Binondo, Tutuban.
2. **E-trike / pedicab** — block-to-block; **agree the fare before boarding**.
3. **Walk** — mall to Tabora is steps; to Binondo ~10 min.

## 5. Tips
1. **Agree trike fare up front** — no meters on pedicabs.
2. **Carry exact fare** for jeepneys; they rarely have change.
3. **Keep bag in front** even on a short ride.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: How do I get from the mall to Binondo?** A: Walk (~10 min) or a short jeepney/e-trike; walking is often easiest.
- **Q: Do e-trikes use meters?** A: Usually no — agree the fare before you board.
- **Q: How much is a jeepney?** A: Minimal (₱13+ standard minimum, ballpark); pay exact.
