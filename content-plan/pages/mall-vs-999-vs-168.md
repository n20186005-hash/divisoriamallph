## 3. The three-hub strategy
Don't assume one building has everything. **Divisoria Mall** = organized, air-con, mixed goods. **999 Mall** = huge open wholesale, clothes/home. **168 Mall** = bargain bins, gifts, accessories. Walk the triangle; compare before buying big.

## 5. Tips
1. **Price-check across two hubs** before committing to bulk.
2. **Use the A/C mall to regroup** between the open-air ones.
3. **Mind the walk** — bags get heavy; consider a trolley or taxi between hubs.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Which is closest to Tabora St?** A: Divisoria Mall is on Tabora itself; 999 and 168 are a 5–10 min walk.
- **Q: Which is air-conditioned?** A: Divisoria Mall offers the most A/C comfort; 999/168 are largely open.
- **Q: Are prices the same?** A: Roughly similar wholesale; always compare across hubs for bulk buys.
