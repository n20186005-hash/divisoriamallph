## 3. Strategy
The **Plaza Lorenzo Ruiz** with its Chinese gate arch is Binondo's symbolic entrance — and the cue that you've arrived at the food district. Use it as the photo op and orientation point before diving into Ongpin.

## 4. What to see (examples)
1. **The gate arch** — the classic Binondo photo.
2. **The plaza & statue** — San Lorenzo Ruiz, first Filipino saint.
3. **Ongpin St begins here** — your food-crawl start line.

## 5. Tips
1. **Shoot the arch from across the street** for the full frame.
2. **Start the food walk here** — Ongpin runs off the plaza.
3. **Mind crossing traffic** — it's a busy intersection.
