## 3. Strategy
Escolta was Manila's pre-war commercial spine — now a **heritage stroll** of art-deco and neoclassical façades, indie cafes, and weekend markets. It's a breather from Divisoria's chaos and pairs well with Binondo.

## 4. What to see (examples)
1. **First United Building & old banks** — restored façades.
2. **Weekend pop-up markets** — check schedules.
3. **Cafes & galleries** — rest stops along the way.

## 5. Tips
1. **Wear good shoes** — it's a walking route.
2. **Bring a camera** — the architecture is the draw.
3. **Combine with Binondo** rather than backtracking through the market.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: How do I get to Escolta from Divisoria?** A: About a 15–20 minute walk or a short jeep from Tabora.
- **Q: Is it worth visiting?** A: Yes if you like heritage architecture and calmer streets after the market.
- **Q: Any cost?** A: Free to walk; cafes/markets are optional.
