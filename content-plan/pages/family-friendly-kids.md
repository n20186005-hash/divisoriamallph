## 3. Strategy
Divisoria with kids is **doable but planned**. Lean on the **mall's A/C** for rests and restrooms, keep the open-air market short, and use Binondo's milder streets for the "fun" part (food, church, arch photo).

## 4. What works (examples)
1. **Mall A/C + food court** — the family base.
2. **Binondo short walk** — church, arch, dumplings kids usually like.
3. **Toys & snacks** — see `/toys-and-party-needs` and `/milk-tea-and-snacks-tabora`.

## 5. Tips
1. **Use a carrier, not a stroller**, on Tabora.
2. **Set a meet-up point** — signal dies in crowds.
3. **Build in snack breaks** to reset tired kids.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Is Divisoria stroller-friendly?** A: The open market isn't — use the mall A/C and a carrier; go early.
- **Q: Where can kids rest?** A: The mall's air-con sections and food court are the easy resets.
- **Q: What food do kids like here?** A: Binondo noodles/dumplings, milk tea, and street snacks.
