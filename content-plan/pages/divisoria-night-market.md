## 3. Strategy
On night-market days, Tabora and nearby streets transform after dark into **open-air evening stalls** — clothes, accessories, snacks. It's lively but denser; keep the same bag-security habits and plan your ride home in advance (rides get scarce late).

## 4. What to expect (examples)
1. **Evening apparel & accessory stalls** — the core night market.
2. **Street food at night** — see `/divisoria-shopping-budget-eateries-and-street-food`.
3. **Crowds peak after 18:00** — go earlier for space.

## 5. Tips
1. **Secure bags in front** — nights are the most crowded.
2. **Arrange transport home** before it gets late.
3. **Carry small bills** — change runs short at night.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Is there a night market at Divisoria?** A: Yes on scheduled evenings (often weekends/holidays) along Tabora and nearby streets.
- **Q: Is it safe at night?** A: Generally busy and safe, but use front-bag habits and plan your ride home.
- **Q: What's different from daytime?** A: More stalls, bigger crowds, and a livelier street-food scene.
