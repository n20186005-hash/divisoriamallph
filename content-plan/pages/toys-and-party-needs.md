## 3. Strategy
Divisoria covers **birthday and event needs**: balloons, piñatas, party favors, wrapping, and general toys. Buy **decor + favors together** to save trips, and keep small toys away from toddlers (choking risk).

## 4. What to look for (examples)
1. **Balloons & piñatas** — by piece or bulk.
2. **Party favors & loot bags** — per pack.
3. **General toys** — check age labels; avoid tiny parts for young kids.

## 5. Tips
1. **Check age grading** on toy packaging for safety.
2. **Inflate balloons at home** — inflated ones are hard to carry.
3. **Bundle with craft supplies** from `/tabora-street-crafts-supplies`.
