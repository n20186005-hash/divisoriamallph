## 3. Strategy
Divisoria is safe to visit but **crowds are where theft happens**. The rule: **your bag is always in front of you and one hand is on it.** Treat cash and phone as things someone else wants in a press of bodies.

## 4. Habits (examples)
1. **Zippered front-crossbody** — not a sling on your back.
2. **Split cash** — only what you'll spend in an easy pocket; rest deeper.
3. **Buddy system** — one holds bags while the other queues.

## 5. Tips
1. **Ignore "accidental" spills or requests** that pull your attention away from your bag.
2. **Use small bills** — flashing a thick wad invites attention.
3. **Set a meet-up point** — signal dies in crowds; agree a spot face-to-face.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Is Divisoria safe for tourists?** A: Yes, but crowded — use front-bag habits and stay alert in queues.
- **Q: How should I carry cash?** A: Small bills in an easy pocket; keep the bulk hidden and split from your bag.
- **Q: What if I'm alone?** A: Wear the bag in front, avoid displaying valuables, and take breaks in A/C zones.
