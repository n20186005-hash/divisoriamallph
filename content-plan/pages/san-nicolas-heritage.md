## 3. Strategy
San Nicolas is the historic trading district wrapped around Divisoria — older than the mall, full of **ancestral commercial buildings** and the memory of Manila's wholesale past. It adds depth to a shopping day without leaving the neighborhood.

## 4. What to see (examples)
1. **Old commercial façades** — Spanish-era and early-20th-century warehouses.
2. **Ancestral shops** — some still trading in original spaces.
3. **Narrow streets** — the texture of old Manila wholesale.

## 5. Tips
1. **Look up** — the interesting architecture is above the ground-floor stalls.
2. **Stay on public streets** — many buildings are private.
3. **Pair with Escolta/Binondo** for a fuller heritage loop.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Where is San Nicolas relative to Divisoria?** A: It borders the market — about 5–10 minutes from Tabora St.
- **Q: Can I go inside the old buildings?** A: Mostly no — view from the street; some have ground-floor shops.
- **Q: Is it safe to walk?** A: Yes, daylight and busy; keep bag-security habits from `/safety-pickpockets-divisoria`.
