## 3. Strategy
The half-day plan **front-loads shopping** (cooler, emptier mornings) and ends with Binondo as the reward. Keep bags in front; buy **pasalubong last** so boxes ride home upright.

## 4. Tips
1. **Start by 08:30** — mornings are the whole game.
2. **Use the mall A/C as your reset** mid-loop.
3. **Bookend with food** — quick Binondo lunch before the walk back.

## 5. FAQ
- **Q: Can I do Divisoria + Binondo in half a day?** A: Yes — 4–5 hours covers a focused shop plus a Binondo food/heritage bite.
- **Q: Best start time?** A: Around 08:30 to beat heat and crowds.
- **Q: How do I avoid backtracking?** A: Shop Tabora first, walk once into Binondo, return from there.
