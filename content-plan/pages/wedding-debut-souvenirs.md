## 3. Strategy
Divisoria is the Philippines' go-to for **event souvenirs** — wedding and 18th-birthday ("debut") tokens by the hundred. Think **personalized trinkets, candles, soaps, tumblers, pouches**. Buy plain in bulk, then personalize on-site or nearby.

## 4. What to look for (examples)
1. **Personalized tokens** — engraved/printed items; ask MOQ.
2. **Candles & soaps** — classic wedding favors.
3. **Pouches & boxes** — for packing your own.

## 5. Tips
1. **Confirm minimum order & lead time** — personalization isn't instant.
2. **Inspect a sample** before approving a bulk run.
3. **Budget for packing supplies** — boxes/ribbon from `/tabora-street-crafts-supplies`.
