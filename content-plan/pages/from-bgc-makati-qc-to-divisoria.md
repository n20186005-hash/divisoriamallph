## 2. Outline
| Origin | Typical route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| BGC / Makati | MRT → LRT-1 → Doroteo Jose, then walk/jeep | ~45–75 min, depends on traffic |
| Quezon City | LRT-2 → Recto, or jeep/bus via Quiapo | ~45–90 min |
| Anywhere | Ride-hailing to "Divisoria Mall, Tabora St" | Costly but door-to-door |

## 3. Strategy
Train is cheapest and avoids Divisoria traffic; **LRT-1 Doroteo Jose** is the key interchange (see `/lrt-1-doroteo-jose-exit`). From there it's a short walk or jeep to Tabora St. Ride-hailing drops you closer but costs more and still hits traffic near the market.

## 4. Options (examples)
1. **MRT-3 → LRT-1 (Doroteo Jose)** — then walk/jeep to Tabora.
2. **LRT-2 (Recto)** — short jeep/trike to Divisoria.
3. **Ride-hailing** — set drop-off to the mall's Tabora St entrance.

## 5. Tips
1. **Travel off-peak** (avoid 7–9 & 17–19) — trains and roads clog.
2. **Keep bags front-facing** on crowded trains.
3. **Have the mall address ready** for drivers: 866 Tabora St, Binondo.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Nearest train station to Divisoria Mall?** A: LRT-1 Doroteo Jose, then a short walk/jeep to Tabora St.
- **Q: How long from Makati?** A: Roughly 45–75 minutes by train; longer by road at peak.
- **Q: Is ride-hailing worth it?** A: For groups or heavy hauls, yes — but expect traffic near the market.
