## 3. Strategy
Divisoria's open-air sections are **low-lying** and pool water in heavy rain. The wet season brings **afternoon thunderstorms** — shop in the morning and clear out before they hit. The A/C mall stays dry and is your refuge.

## 4. What to expect (examples)
1. **Tabora open stretch** — first to flood; wear sandals, not closed shoes.
2. **Afternoon downpours** — predictable in wet months; plan around them.
3. **Mall as shelter** — stay dry, shop indoors, wait it out.

## 5. Tips
1. **Wear quick-dry footwear** and a packable rain jacket.
2. **Protect purchases** — bring a waterproof tote or liner.
3. **Check the forecast** and aim for a morning trip in wet months.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: When is Divisoria flood-prone?** A: Mainly the rainy season (roughly Jun–Oct); open Tabora stretches pool first.
- **Q: Best time of year to visit?** A: Dry months (roughly Dec–May) are most comfortable.
- **Q: What if it rains while I'm there?** A: Use the A/C mall as shelter and protect bags with a waterproof liner.
