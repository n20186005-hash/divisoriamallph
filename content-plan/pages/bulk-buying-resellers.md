## 3. Strategy
Resellers win in Divisoria by **volume discipline**: compare across the three hubs, confirm minimums and defect rates, and **inspect a sample** before approving a bulk run. The open-air hubs (999/168) usually beat the mall on per-unit price; the mall wins on comfort and security.

## 4. What works (examples)
1. **Per-dozen wholesale racks** — fixed, no haggle.
2. **Cross-hub price checks** — see `/mall-vs-999-vs-168`.
3. **Bulk souvenirs/toys** — see `/wedding-debut-souvenirs` and `/toys-and-party-needs`.

## 5. Tips
1. **Negotiate on volume**, not on single pieces.
2. **Inspect seams/zippers/flaws** before bulk commitment.
3. **Arrange transport** — loads get heavy; use a trolley or taxi.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: How do I buy wholesale in Divisoria?** A: Ask per-dozen/pack pricing, compare hubs, and inspect samples before bulk.
- **Q: Which mall is cheapest for resellers?** A: 999/168 open hubs usually beat the mall per unit; the mall is pricier but safer/A/C.
- **Q: Any risks?** A: Counterfeit branded goods and un-inspected defects — buy unbranded and check quality first.
