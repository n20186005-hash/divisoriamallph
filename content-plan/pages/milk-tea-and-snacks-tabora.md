## 3. The grab-and-go strategy
Tabora St is a snack corridor: **milk-tea franchises and local kiosks**, plus **turon, banana cue and packaged snacks** sold by the bag. These are the between-stalls energy source — not a meal, just momentum.

## 4. What to grab (examples)
1. **Milk-tea kiosks** — ₱50–₱120; choose sealed cups you can carry.
2. **Turon / banana cue carts** — ₱10–₱20; eat immediately, greasy fingers.
3. **Packaged snacks & pasalay (chicharon, nuts)** — by the bag for take-home; check seals.

## 5. Tips
1. **One hand on the drink, one on the bag** — don't set a tote down to free a hand.
2. **Prefer sealed, branded packs** for pasalubong; avoid loose repacked items.
3. **Carry small bills** — kiosks are cash-first and slow with change.
