## 3. Strategy
Divisoria is Manila's Christmas HQ. The **parol** (star lantern) is the icon, plus fairy lights, ornaments and tree supplies. It's a **seasonal crush** — go early in the season, and protect fragile items in a rigid tote.

## 4. What to look for (examples)
1. **Parol** — handmade star lanterns in many sizes.
2. **Fairy lights & wiring** — check plug type and length.
3. **Ornaments & tree skirts** — by the box for bulk.

## 5. Tips
1. **Test lights before buying** if the stall allows.
2. **Carry a rigid box** for parol/glass ornaments.
3. **Go early season** — best stock sells out by mid-December.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: When is Christmas stock available?** A: Roughly November to January; peak choice is early season.
- **Q: Where are parol sold?** A: Along Tabora St and inside Divisoria's malls.
- **Q: How do I carry fragile décor?** A: In a separate rigid tote, bought last in your trip.
