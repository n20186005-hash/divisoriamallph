## 3. The chill-stop strategy
Divisoria is hot, crowded and sensory-overloading. A cafe isn't a luxury here — it's **logistics**: a place to sit, hydrate, count your purchases, and agree on the next move. Prioritise **air-conditioning** over ambience.

## 4. Where to stop (examples)
1. **In-mall / Lucky Chinatown Mall cafes** — air-con, clean restrooms, card-friendly; ideal first regroup.
2. **Milk-tea kiosks along Tabora** — quick sugar hit, grab-and-go (see `/milk-tea-and-snacks-tabora`).
3. **Binondo coffee bars (Ongpin/Carvajal)** — sit-down, slower; better as a post-shopping wind-down.

## 5. Tips
1. **Pick a cafe with a visible queue of locals** — it's a safe, legitimate restroom and recharge stop.
2. **Hold bags on your lap or between feet** — don't prop a tote on an empty chair.
3. **Set the next meet-up time before you sit** — signal dies in crowds; decide in person.
