## 3. Strategy
Divisoria is Manila's fabric capital. The **Tabora/open-air textile stretch** sells cotton, polyester, lace, and native fabrics by the yard at wholesale rates. Bring a **tape measure mental note** and buy a little extra for shrinkage/hems.

## 4. What to look for (examples)
1. **Cotton / polyester bolts** — everyday sewing, ₱30–₱120/yd.
2. **Lace & trims** — by the metre for garments and crafts.
3. **Native / specialty cloth** — check authenticity and weave.

## 5. Tips
1. **Check the bolt end for width & flaws** before committing.
2. **Buy 10–20% extra** for hems and mistakes.
3. **Ask for the "tindahan" (retail) price** if buying less than a bolt — wholesale is per full piece.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Where do I buy fabric in Divisoria?** A: Along Tabora St and adjacent blocks, plus the mall's textile section.
- **Q: How much is cloth per yard?** A: Ballpark ₱30–₱300+ depending on type; confirm per stall.
- **Q: Can I get it tailored nearby?** A: Yes, tailors operate around Divisoria — ask stall owners for referrals.
