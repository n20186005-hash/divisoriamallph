## 3. Strategy
**Doroteo Jose** is the LRT-1 stop that connects you to Divisoria. From the station, head toward **Recto/Tabora**; it's walkable but with heavy hauls a jeep or e-trike covers the last stretch. This is the backbone commute for most shoppers.

## 4. Route (examples)
1. **Alight at Doroteo Jose** — follow signs toward Recto/Tabora side.
2. **Walk ~10–15 min** or take a jeepney/e-trike to the mall.
3. **Return the same way** — station is well signed back to LRT-1.

## 5. Tips
1. **Board with bags in front** — trains get packed.
2. **Buy a stored-value card** if doing multiple LRT trips.
3. **Walk if light, ride if loaded** — the last stretch is short either way.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Which LRT station is nearest Divisoria Mall?** A: LRT-1 Doroteo Jose; then ~10–15 min walk or short jeep to Tabora St.
- **Q: Can I bring shopping on the train?** A: Yes — keep bags in front and avoid rush hour.
- **Q: Walking or riding from the station?** A: Walk if light (~10–15 min); ride if you have heavy hauls.
