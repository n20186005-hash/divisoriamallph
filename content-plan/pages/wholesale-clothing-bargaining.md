## 3. Strategy
Clothes are Divisoria's backbone — **wholesale tees, dresses, underwear, native wear**. Bargaining is expected on loose/retail buys; true wholesale is fixed per dozen. Know the difference.

## 4. What to look for (examples)
1. **Per-dozen wholesale racks** — fixed price, no haggle.
2. **Loose/retail piles** — room to negotiate.
3. **Native & costume pieces** — barong, sayá, seasonal.

## 5. Tips
1. **Ask "presyo pakete?" (per dozen)** vs single price.
2. **Check seams, zippers, stains** before paying.
3. **Caution on branded goods** — counterfeit items carry legal/quality risk; buy unbranded.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Do I bargain in Divisoria?** A: Yes for loose/retail buys; per-dozen wholesale is usually fixed.
- **Q: Where's the best clothing wholesale?** A: 999 and 168 Malls plus Tabora; compare across hubs.
- **Q: Are branded items safe to buy?** A: Unbranded is safer; "designer" goods here are often counterfeit — buy at your own risk.
