## 3. The in-mall option
When the street heat or the crowd gets to you, the mall's own food area is the low-friction choice: **air-conditioning, clean restrooms, and card-friendly counters**. It's pricier and less "authentic" than Tabora or Binondo, but unbeatable for a **secure, cool pit stop** with your bags in sight.

## 4. What you'll find (examples)
1. **Food-court stalls** — rice meals, noodles, snacks; consistent, sanitized, air-con.
2. **Snack/dessert kiosks** — halo-halo, turon, milk tea near entrances.
3. **A/C seating** — the real value: a place to sit with bags on your lap and regroup.

## 5. Tips
1. **Use it mid-shop, not as your main meal** — save the Binondo crawl for the "real" food.
2. **Keep bags in your eyeline** — even indoors, don't leave a tote on a chair.
3. **Top up water here** — hydration matters more than saving ₱20 on the street.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Is there food inside Divisoria Mall?** A: Yes — a food court / snack area with air-con seating and restrooms.
- **Q: Is it cheaper than outside?** A: Usually a bit pricier than street food, but cooler and more secure.
- **Q: When should I eat here vs. Binondo?** A: Use the mall for a quick in-building pit stop; go to Binondo for the proper food crawl.
