## 3. Strategy
Binondo Church (Minor Basilica of San Lorenzo Ruiz) is the historic heart of Chinatown and a natural **anchor** for a Divisoria + Binondo day. It's free, photogenic, and a logical place to pivot from shopping to the food walk.

## 4. What to see (examples)
1. **The basilica façade & interior** — rebuilt after wartime damage.
2. **Plaza Lorenzo Ruiz** — the square out front (see `/plaza-lorenzo-ruiz-arch`).
3. **Nearby Ongpin** — the food strip begins here.

## 5. Tips
1. **Dress modestly** if you'll step inside.
2. **Go weekday morning** to avoid Sunday mass crowds.
3. **Photo from the plaza** — the front view is the classic shot.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: How far is Binondo Church from Divisoria Mall?** A: About a 10–15 minute walk from the mall's Tabora St side into Binondo.
- **Q: Is there an entrance fee?** A: No — it's a working church, free to visit.
- **Q: Can I combine it with food?** A: Yes — it's the natural turnaround point of the Binondo food crawl.
