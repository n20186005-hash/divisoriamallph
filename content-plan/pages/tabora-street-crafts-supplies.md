## 3. Strategy
Tabora St is the craft-and-sundries spine of Divisoria: **ribbons, artificial flowers, balloons, DIY, gift wrap, candles**. It's the first thing you hit leaving the mall — do it before the heat and crowds build.

## 4. What to look for (examples)
1. **Ribbon & wrap rows** — by the roll; cheaper per metre in bulk.
2. **Artificial flowers / foam** — for events and décor.
3. **DIY & school craft supplies** — beads, glue, boards.

## 5. Tips
1. **Negotiate on volume** — ask "how much for 10 / a whole pack?"
2. **Keep receipts and count before leaving** — easy to lose track at busy stalls.
3. **Wear a front-crossbody bag** — Tabora sidewalks are tight and distraction-theft prone.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Where are craft supplies in Divisoria?** A: Along Tabora St immediately outside Divisoria Mall, plus inside the mall.
- **Q: Can I buy in bulk?** A: Yes — most stalls sell per roll/pack with lower per-unit prices in volume.
- **Q: Best day to go?** A: Weekday mornings are cooler and less crowded.
