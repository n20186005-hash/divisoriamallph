## 3. Strategy
On wet days, **stay inside the connected malls** — Divisoria Mall, 999 and 168 have covered floors. Skip the open Tabora lanes (they pool water first) and use **covered walkways/jeepney hops** between buildings. Keep a waterproof tote liner for anything you do buy.

## 4. What works (examples)
1. **Mall A/C shopping** — fully covered.
2. **999/168 indoor sections** — big, dry floor plates.
3. **Short covered jeepney hops** between hubs.

## 5. Tips
1. **Bring a packable rain jacket + waterproof tote liner.**
2. **Wear sandals**, not closed shoes, if you must step outside.
3. **Check the forecast** — aim for a morning trip before afternoon storms.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Can I shop Divisoria in the rain?** A: Yes — stay inside the malls; skip open Tabora lanes that flood first.
- **Q: Which parts are dry?** A: Divisoria Mall and the 999/168 indoor sections are covered.
- **Q: How do I move between buildings when wet?** A: Use covered walkways and short jeepney hops; protect bags with a liner.
