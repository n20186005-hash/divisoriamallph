## 3. Strategy
Driving to Divisoria is doable but **parking is the bottleneck**. Use a **paid lot** near the mall rather than hunting street space. Arrive early; on weekends and paydays it's a scramble.

## 4. Options (examples)
1. **Mall-adjacent pay lots** — closest, priciest, most secure.
2. **Tutuban-area lots** — more spaces, slightly farther.
3. **Street parking** — scarce and often tow/blocked; not recommended.

## 5. Tips
1. **Don't leave visible shopping** in the car — secure the trunk.
2. **Take a parking stub photo** — easy to forget level/zone.
3. **Prefer lots with attendants** over dark side streets.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Is there parking at Divisoria Mall?** A: Paid lots nearby; on-site/street space is limited — arrive early.
- **Q: How much is parking?** A: Ballpark ₱40–₱100+ per entry/hour; confirm per lot.
- **Q: Is it safe to leave purchases in the car?** A: Stow them in the trunk out of sight; use attended lots.
