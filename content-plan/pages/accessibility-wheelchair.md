## 3. Strategy
**The mall's A/C sections are your friend** — step-free, elevators, restrooms. The **open-air Tabora market is the challenge**: uneven pavement, curbs, and crowding make wheelchairs and strollers tough. Plan to do most accessible shopping inside, and treat the street as a short, assisted visit.

## 4. What to expect (examples)
1. **Mall elevators & wide aisles** — manageable.
2. **Tabora sidewalk crowds** — need a helper; go weekday, early.
3. **Tutuban/999/168** — bigger floor plates but busy; quieter off-peak.

## 5. Tips
1. **Go weekday morning** — fewest obstacles and people.
2. **Use the mall as base** — step-free rest and restrooms.
3. **Bring a helper** for curb cuts and crowd gaps on the street.

## 6. FAQ
- **Q: Is Divisoria Mall wheelchair accessible?** A: The A/C mall has step-free entries and elevators — the easiest part.
- **Q: Can I use a stroller at Tabora St?** A: It's hard — narrow, uneven, crowded; go early with a helper.
- **Q: Best time for accessibility?** A: Weekday mornings, using the mall as your accessible base.
