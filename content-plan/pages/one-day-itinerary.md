## 3. Strategy
A full day lets you **compare the three wholesale hubs**, do Binondo properly, and add a heritage walk without rushing. The rule remains: **shop morning, eat/historic afternoon, pasalubong last.**

## 4. Tips
1. **Pack light, buy a trolley** if going heavy.
2. **Schedule A/C resets** — don't push through the heat.
3. **Confirm transport home** before night market crowds.

## 5. FAQ
- **Q: Can one day cover everything?** A: Yes — ~8–9 hours covers wholesale, Binondo and a heritage walk at a relaxed pace.
- **Q: How do I avoid exhaustion?** A: Build in mall A/C resets and a proper Binondo lunch.
- **Q: What's the must-not-skip?** A: The Binondo food crawl and buying pasalubong last.
